# Phase 2 engineering coverage audit

Coverage classifies the usability of the staged guidance. Source conditions and runtime verification are tracked separately. COMPLETE means the workflow provides practical decisions and checks. PARTIAL indicates an unresolved implementation or verification question in the scope. NOT_PRESENT_IN_SOURCE and REQUIRES_MORE_EVIDENCE qualify source findings. NOT_APPLICABLE applies to this reference's absent product requirements. Evidence anchors refer to the [source registry](SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md). The approved [React audit](PHASE_1_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md) remains separate.
