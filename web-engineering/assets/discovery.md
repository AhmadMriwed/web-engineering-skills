# Project discovery worksheet

- Objective, audience, roles, priority journeys and acceptance criteria
- Accessibility, localization, SEO, security, performance and operating constraints
- Existing framework, dependency versions, APIs, infrastructure and conventions
- Persistence, identity, permissions, storage, realtime, jobs and integrations
- Explicit requirements, implied constraints, unresolved material decisions and out-of-scope items
- Viable frontend and backend alternatives, costs, ownership, recommendation and trade-offs
- Approved decisions, assumptions, verification approach and related decision records
